VirtIO support (meta-virtio)
======================================================

meta-virtio provides virtualized I/O (VirtIO) support not available from other
upstream layers (e.g. meta-virtualization).  VirtIO standards are considered a
common framework to provide virtualized devices. Developing standardized
solutions reduces community fragmentation and increases freedom to choose
hypervisor and SoC.
